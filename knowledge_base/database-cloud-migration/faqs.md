# FAQs — Database & Cloud Migration

> Source: Osiyatech Services Knowledge Base, Draft v3.0. Captured 2026-10-26. Source PDF pages 77–80.
> FAQ entries tagged `[drafted]` remain unapproved and must be reviewed.

Can you migrate our database to the cloud with minimal downtime? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech plans migrations to minimise downtime and disruption. The process includes a compatibility
assessment, full backups before any change, data replication and synchronisation so the new environment
stays current during the move, validation and testing, and a phased cut-over, often scheduled for quieter
business hours. The exact downtime depends on your data volume and systems, and is agreed in the migration
plan.
Which databases can you migrate? [drafted]
Osiyatech works with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL and Redis, and migrates to managed cloud targets such
as AWS RDS and Supabase, as well as to databases on AWS, Azure and Google Cloud. Other database
platforms can be assessed on request. ERP and CRM data migrations, such as Pronto to Dynamics 365, are
covered under ERP & CRM Solutions.
How do you make sure no data is lost? [drafted]
Data safety is the top priority. Full backups are taken before migration, data is validated after transfer to confirm
it is complete and accurate, and replication keeps source and target in sync until the cut-over. Testing confirms
that applications work correctly with the new database before the old system is retired. A client testimonial
describes an ERP data migration with zero data loss.
Do you set up backups and disaster recovery? [drafted]
Yes. Backup and disaster recovery is part of the service. Osiyatech configures automated backups and
recovery procedures, so your data can be restored quickly if hardware fails, data is accidentally deleted or
another incident occurs.
Will our database be faster after migration? [drafted]
Often, yes. Migration is a good opportunity to improve performance through database optimisation, query and
index tuning, right-sized cloud resources, and caching with Redis where appropriate. The specific
improvements depend on your current setup, and are identified during assessment.
How much does a migration cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on data volume, the number of databases and
applications, complexity, downtime requirements and whether optimisation or disaster recovery setup is
included. After an assessment, you'll receive a transparent estimate.
