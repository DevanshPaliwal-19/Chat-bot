# FAQs — Enterprise Software Development

What is enterprise software development?
Enterprise software development means designing and building applications that support complex business
operations: internal workflows, data management, approvals, reporting and the processes that connect
departments. Unlike consumer apps or simple websites, enterprise software has to handle many users with
different permissions, integrate with other business systems, protect sensitive data and stay reliable for years.
Osiyatech builds these solutions around each organisation's specific requirements, with scalability, security and
integration designed in from the start.
What types of enterprise software does Osiyatech develop?
Osiyatech develops custom business applications, enterprise portals for employees, customers, vendors and
partners, workflow management and approval systems, internal operations platforms, business rule engines,
legacy modernization solutions and scalable multi-module enterprise applications. These are built for a range of
industries, including legal, manufacturing, civic technology and financial services.
Can you modernize our existing enterprise applications?
Yes. Legacy modernization is a core part of the service. Depending on what your system needs, it can include
upgrading outdated technology, re-architecting monolithic applications into modular or microservice designs,
strengthening security, improving performance, and moving suitable workloads to modern cloud environments.
Osiyatech first assesses the existing application to understand what the business depends on, then plans the
modernization in stages so operations continue throughout.
Can your enterprise software integrate with our existing systems?
Yes. Enterprise applications can connect with your existing ERP, CRM, accounting, inventory, HR and other
business platforms through APIs, middleware and custom integrations. That removes duplicate data entry and
keeps information consistent across systems. For integration-heavy projects, Osiyatech's Enterprise System
Integration service provides additional specialist expertise.
How long does enterprise software development take?
It depends on the complexity, the number of features and modules, the integrations required and the technical
requirements. A focused enterprise application may take a few months. Larger multi-module platforms are
usually delivered in phases: core modules go live first so the business benefits early, and further modules
follow. A detailed roadmap is created during the Discovery stage.
Why choose custom enterprise software instead of off-the-shelf solutions?
Custom enterprise software is built around how your organisation actually works, instead of forcing your teams
to adapt to a generic product. It gives you more flexibility, full control over functionality and integrations, and the
ability to change the software as your business changes. It can also reduce dependence on several
disconnected tools and their licence costs. Off-the-shelf products suit standard processes well, and Osiyatech
will tell you honestly if a packaged solution would serve you better.
Do you provide SLA-based support for enterprise applications? [drafted]
Yes. Maintenance and dedicated SLA support are part of this service. Support covers performance monitoring,
security updates, troubleshooting, bug fixes and continuous enhancements for business-critical applications.
Response times and coverage are agreed in the SLA to match how critical the system is to your operations.

Can you build portals for our customers, vendors or partners? [drafted]
Yes. Enterprise Portals & Business Platforms covers central portals for employees, customers, vendors and
partners. Each portal has role-based access, so every user sees only what is relevant to them, along with
real-time information and connected workflows such as orders, approvals, documents or support requests.
Can you work with our existing technology stack? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech normally recommends the most suitable technology for each project, choosing from Node.js,
Python, Java Spring, Go, .NET, React, Angular and more. If your organisation already has a pre-selected stack
or internal technology standards, Osiyatech can build within them, so the solution fits your existing systems and
your in-house team can maintain it.
How much does enterprise software cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the number of modules, the complexity of workflows,
integrations, user scale and support requirements. For larger or uncertain projects, the Discovery &
Architecture engagement provides a technical audit, architecture blueprint and cost estimate before you commit
to the full build.
