## 1. Official service name
BI & Data Analytics - Interactive dashboards & ETL

## 2. Service overview
Business intelligence dashboards, data integration and ETL, data warehousing, automated reporting and business performance analytics.

## 3. Business problems it solves
- Data spread across disconnected systems
- Manual, error-prone spreadsheet reporting
- Lack of visibility into KPIs and trends
- Data not ready for AI applications

## 4. What Osiyatech provides
- **Custom BI Dashboards** - Interactive dashboards for business KPIs and operational metrics.
- **Data Visualization & Reporting** - Charts, reports and visual analytics.
- **Data Integration & ETL** - Combining data from databases, APIs, cloud platforms and business apps into analysis-ready datasets.
- **Data Warehousing & Modeling** - Structured warehouses and optimized data models.
- **Automated Reporting & Analytics** - Scheduled reports and automated data updates.
- **Business Performance Analytics** - Analysis of sales, customer behaviour, financial and operational metrics.

## 5. Key features and capabilities
- Approach: Data Integration > Dashboard Development > Reporting Automation
- Data governance: cleansing, validation (Services catalog)
- Executive reporting suites (Services catalog)
- AI data readiness: preparing reliable data foundations for analytics and AI

## 6. Technologies and platforms mentioned
- **BI & Visualization:** Power BI, Tableau, Google Looker, Metabase, Grafana
- **Data Warehousing & Lakes:** Google BigQuery, Snowflake, Amazon Redshift, PostgreSQL
- **ETL & Data Pipelines:** Apache Airflow, dbt, Talend, Apache Kafka, Python, Pandas
- **Analytics & Cloud Platforms:** AWS Analytics, Azure Data, Google Cloud, Docker

## 7. Target customers or industries
Organizations that need unified reporting from CRMs, ERPs, databases and marketing platforms.
