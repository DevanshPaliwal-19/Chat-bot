## 1. Official service name

## 2. Service overview
Custom ERP and CRM development, Microsoft Dynamics 365 (Business Central and CRM) implementation and customization, integration, data migration and ongoing support.

## 3. Business problems it solves
- Finance, inventory, procurement and sales data in separate systems
- Legacy ERP/CRM needing migration
- Manual sales, quoting and invoicing
- Need for Dynamics 365 customization

## 4. What Osiyatech provides
- **Custom ERP Development** - ERP for finance, inventory, procurement, operations and reporting.
- **Custom CRM Development** - CRM for leads, sales pipelines, customer interactions and service.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 Solutions** - Implementation, customization and extension of Dynamics 365 Business Central and CRM.
- **ERP & CRM Integration** - Connecting ERP, CRM, accounting, eCommerce and third-party apps via APIs and data sync.
- **Data Migration & Modernization** - Migrating records and operational data from legacy systems with a focus on accuracy and continuity.
- **ERP & CRM Support** - Maintenance, optimization, troubleshooting, upgrades and enhancements.
