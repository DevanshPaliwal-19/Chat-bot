## 1. Official service name
AI & Machine Learning - Predictive & GenAI models

## 2. Service overview
Custom machine learning, generative AI, NLP, computer vision, RAG and AI-powered applications that turn business data into actionable intelligence, with deployment options evaluated per engagement.

## 3. Business problems it solves
- Business data that is not being used for forecasting or decisions
- Manual processing of documents such as invoices and contracts
- Need for AI that answers from company knowledge reliably
- Data-privacy or sovereignty constraints on using third-party AI

## 4. What Osiyatech provides
- **AI Chatbots & Conversational AI** - Context-aware chatbots powered by LLMs and business knowledge.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** - AI that retrieves information from documents, databases and enterprise content for more reliable responses.
- **LLM Fine-Tuning & Private Deployment** - Customizing and deploying LLMs with domain data, with secure hosting options and controlled access.
- **Document Intelligence & OCR** - Extraction, classification and processing of invoices, contracts, forms and other documents.
- **Predictive Analytics & Forecasting** - ML models for demand forecasting, customer behaviour analysis, anomaly detection and planning.
- **Computer Vision & Image Analysis** - Image recognition, object detection, visual inspection and image analysis.
- **Model Evaluation & Prototyping** - Exploring candidate models, prompts, retrieval approaches and evaluation criteria through controlled experiments.
- **Self-Hosted AI Approaches** - Assessing independently operated model inference where control, governance or architecture needs justify it.

## 5. Key features and capabilities
- Generative AI integrations and fine-tuning (Services catalog)
- Natural Language Processing (NLP) and vision
- Time-series forecasting and anomaly detection
- Production ML model deployment and monitoring
- Deployment options: on-premises, private cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP) or hybrid, assessed per engagement

### Delivery stages
1. **Discovery & Feasibility** - Define target workflow, data constraints, success criteria and expected usage.
2. **Architecture Options** - Compare suitable models, integration patterns and hosting approaches.
3. **Prototype & Evaluate** - Run controlled experiments; evaluate quality, latency and operating characteristics.
4. **Production Design** - Scope resilience, security, monitoring, scaling and support requirements.
5. **Deployment & Improvement** - Implement the approved architecture; measure outcomes and iterate.

## 6. Technologies and platforms mentioned
- **LLMs & Generative AI:** OpenAI, Hugging Face, LangChain, LlamaIndex
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning:** PyTorch, TensorFlow, Scikit-learn, Python
- **Vector Databases & Data:** Pinecone, PostgreSQL
- **Backend & Deployment:** FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, Kafka, AWS
- **Open-source models mentioned:** Llama 3, Mistral
