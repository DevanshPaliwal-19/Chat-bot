## 12-13. Customer questions and verified answers

**FAQ-067 - Q: What is your typical UI/UX design process and timeline?**

A: The process follows the Double Diamond method: Discovery & User Research (weeks 1-2), Information Architecture & Wireframing (weeks 2-3), High-Fidelity UI Design Systems (weeks 3-5) and Interactive Prototyping & Usability Validation (weeks 5-6). A typical MVP or SaaS redesign takes 4 to 8 weeks depending on screen count and workflow complexity.

Alternative questions: How long does a UI/UX project take? | What is your design process?

Source: https://ayushitated.com/ui-ux-design

**FAQ-068 - Q: What deliverables will we receive at the end of a UI/UX project?**

A: The client receives the organized Figma workspace: user flow maps, clickable high-fidelity prototypes, a design system component kit with documented variants and tokens, icon sets, responsive desktop/tablet/mobile artboards, and developer handoff specifications.

Alternative questions: What do I get from the design project? | Do you hand over Figma files?

Source: https://ayushitated.com/ui-ux-design

**FAQ-069 - Q: How do you ensure UI/UX designs translate cleanly into code?**

A: Designs use auto-layout that mirrors CSS Flexbox and Grid, an 8pt spacing scale and semantic design tokens that correspond to Tailwind CSS, React or Flutter variables. Live design-to-engineering handoff walkthroughs are also held.

Alternative questions: Will developers be able to build your designs? | How is design handoff done?

Source: https://ayushitated.com/ui-ux-design

**FAQ-070 - Q: Can you redesign our existing application without disrupting current users?**

A: Yes. Osiyatech uses progressive UX modernization: identifying friction points, simplifying forms, improving visual hierarchy and rolling out refreshed components incrementally to preserve familiarity for existing users.

Alternative questions: Can you redesign our app gradually? | Will a redesign confuse our users?

**FAQ-071 - Q: Do you conduct usability testing with actual target users?**

A: Yes, depending on project scope. Moderated or unmoderated usability tests are run on interactive prototypes before code is written, recording task completion, time-on-task and confusion areas.
