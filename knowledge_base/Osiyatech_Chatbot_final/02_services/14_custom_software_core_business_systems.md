## 1. Official service name

## 2. Service overview
Replacing off-the-shelf software and spreadsheets with custom ERP, CRM and internal operations platforms built around how the company operates. Linked from the Home page 'Enterprise Systems & ERP' card; not listed in the Services catalog.

## 3. Business problems it solves
- Rigid off-the-shelf software
- Fragile spreadsheet-based processes
- Disconnected accounting, billing and shipping systems

## 4. What Osiyatech provides
- **Custom ERP & Operations Portals** - Operations engines unifying inventory, accounting, project milestones and staff permissions.
- **Microsoft Dynamics 365 & Integration** - Business Central extensions, Power Platform connectors and legacy ERP sync.
- **Workflow Automation & Event Engines** - Document generation, multi-tier approvals, webhook triggers and audit-logged signing pipelines.
- **Bespoke CRM & Client Portals** - Tracking deals, quotes, deliverables and support tickets.
- **Legacy System Modernization** - Refactoring monolithic databases and desktop software into cloud-native web backends.
- **API Integration & Middleware Bridges** - Connecting accounting, billing, shipping and marketing APIs.
- **Enterprise Security & RBAC** - Role-based permissions, audit trails, SSO and encryption at rest.
