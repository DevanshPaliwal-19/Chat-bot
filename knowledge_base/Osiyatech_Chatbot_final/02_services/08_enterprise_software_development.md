Custom enterprise applications, portals, workflow automation, legacy modernization, enterprise architecture and ongoing maintenance for business-critical systems.

## 3. Business problems it solves
- Complex internal operations handled by disconnected tools
- Outdated legacy applications
- Repetitive manual approvals and processes
- Off-the-shelf software that does not fit workflows

## 4. What Osiyatech provides
- **Custom Enterprise Applications** - Applications that streamline internal operations and automate processes across departments.
- **Enterprise Portals & Business Platforms** - Employee, customer, vendor and partner portals with role-based access.
- **Legacy Application Modernization** - Turning outdated applications into secure, scalable, maintainable systems.
- **Business Process & Workflow Automation** - Approval systems, event-driven operations and business rule engines.
- **Enterprise Application Architecture** - Modular architectures using microservices and API-first development.
- **Enterprise Software Maintenance & Support** - Maintenance, monitoring, security updates, troubleshooting and enhancements.
