# Hire Experts: Trial, Replacement and Collaboration

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Document IDs | POLICY-017 |
| Category | Policies |
| Source URL | https://ayushitated.com/hire-experts |
| Verification Status | Documented |
| Last Reviewed | 2026-11-02 |

- 14-day zero-risk trial period; if the developer does not meet technical or speed expectations, an immediate senior replacement is provided at zero downtime or additional cost.
- Minimum 4-6 hours daily working overlap with US (EST/PST), UK/Europe (GMT/CET), Middle East (GST) and APAC business hours.
- Engagement models: Dedicated Full-Time (160 hrs/month); Dedicated Part-Time (80 hrs/month); Full Agile Engineering Squad (Tech Lead, Senior Developers, UI/UX Designer, dedicated QA Engineer); Technical Lead / Architecture Advisory.
- Onboarding: 48-72 hours for individual developers; 1-2 weeks for squads.
