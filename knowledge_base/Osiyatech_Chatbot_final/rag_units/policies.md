## [POLICY-013] Intellectual property
**Subcategory:** Documented (service-level)

**Related Questions:** What is your intellectual property?

Clients retain 100% IP ownership of source code, architecture documentation, design files and database schemas created by Osiyatech developers 'from day one' (Hire Experts); Figma files, research and design tokens on completion with no lock-in or licensing fees (UI/UX); source code, design systems and schemas, with apps on the client's store accounts (Mobile). Home page: 'ownership of agreed project deliverables'.

**Source:** https://ayushitated.com/hire-experts ; https://ayushitated.com/ui-ux-design ; https://ayushitated.com/mobile-development ; https://ayushitated.com/
