Service overview
Osiyatech's BI & Data Analytics service helps businesses turn complex data into smarter decisions. The tagline
is "Turn Your Business Data into Smarter Decisions." The service builds custom business intelligence (BI)
dashboards, data visualisations, automated reports and data integration pipelines, so leadership and teams
can track performance, spot trends and find growth opportunities.
Business intelligence means collecting, integrating, analysing and presenting business data so decisions are
based on evidence, not guesswork. Most organisations hold data in many places: CRMs, ERPs, databases,
marketing platforms, spreadsheets and third-party apps. Osiyatech connects these sources through automated
ETL (extract, transform, load) pipelines, models the data in a warehouse, and presents it in interactive
dashboards built around each business's own KPIs.
The homepage describes this area as "Data Engineering & BI": automated data pipelines, warehouse
modelling on PostgreSQL and BigQuery, and clear dashboards leadership teams can actually make decisions
with. A common starting point is replacing fragile, manual Excel reporting with automated pipelines and live
dashboards.
Business problems solved
•
Data scattered across many systems: with no single view of the business, numbers have to be pieced
together by hand. Osiyatech integrates CRMs, ERPs, databases, marketing platforms and APIs into unified
dashboards.
•
Manual spreadsheet reporting: time-consuming Excel reports are error-prone and quickly out of date.
Automated pipelines and dashboards save many hours every week and remove manual calculation errors.
•
Decisions made on guesswork: without clear KPIs, leaders can't see what is working. Custom dashboards
show performance, revenue trends and operational bottlenecks.
•
Stale information: reports produced weekly or monthly arrive too late to act on. Refresh cadences range
from real-time streaming to scheduled hourly, daily or weekly updates.
•
Uncontrolled access to sensitive data: row-level security and role-based access ensure each person
sees only the data they should.
•
No forward view: predictive analytics and forecasting help anticipate demand and outcomes.

Target customers and use cases
BI & Data Analytics serves organisations that have data but struggle to turn it into clear, timely decisions,
usually because the data is spread across systems or locked in manual spreadsheets.
By company size
•
Startups: growing companies that need clear visibility of key metrics, such as revenue, user growth and
retention, without building a data team. Lightweight dashboards with tools such as Metabase or custom
React dashboards give founders and investors a reliable view of performance.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): businesses whose managers spend hours each week compiling
Excel reports from different systems. Osiyatech automates these reports and replaces them with live
dashboards, saving time and removing manual errors.
•
Enterprises: large organisations with many systems, such as CRMs, ERPs, databases and marketing
platforms, that need a central data warehouse, governed access and consistent reporting across
departments. They benefit from scalable ETL pipelines, row-level security and real-time or scheduled
refreshes.
By situation or need
•
Data spread across systems: organisations using Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP, Dynamics, several
databases and marketing tools that need one unified view.
