What is business intelligence (BI), and how does it help my business?
Business intelligence is the combination of software and analysis methods used to collect, integrate, analyse
and present business data in a clear, usable form, typically as interactive dashboards and reports. It replaces
guesswork with evidence. Leaders can see revenue trends, spot operational bottlenecks, track KPIs and
identify opportunities, then make confident, data-backed decisions. With automated BI, this information stays
current without anyone compiling reports by hand.
Can you integrate data from multiple sources into a single dashboard?
Yes, this is one of Osiyatech's core strengths. The team connects separate systems, including CRMs
(Salesforce, HubSpot), ERPs (SAP, Microsoft Dynamics), SQL and NoSQL databases, marketing platforms,
spreadsheets and third-party APIs, through automated ETL pipelines into a central data model or warehouse.
The result is one consistent source of truth and dashboards that show the full picture across your business.
Which BI tools and platforms do you support?
Osiyatech builds solutions with Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Google Looker, Metabase and Grafana, as well as
custom React-based dashboards for needs beyond standard tools. The choice depends on your existing
technology, licensing preferences, budget and who will use the dashboards. If you already use a BI tool,
Osiyatech can build on it. If you're starting fresh, the team will recommend the best fit.
How often can our dashboards and reports be refreshed?
Refresh frequency is set to match your needs. For live operations, real-time streaming using Kafka or
WebSockets keeps dashboards updated within moments, with Redis holding fast-changing metrics. For most
reporting, automated scheduled refreshes, hourly, daily or weekly, run through reliable pipelines built with tools
such as Apache Airflow or dbt. Different dashboards can use different refresh rates.
How do you handle data security and access governance?
Access to data is carefully controlled. Osiyatech applies row-level security (RLS), so each user sees only the
rows they're permitted to see, such as their own region or department. It also applies role-based access control
(RBAC), and encryption of data in transit and at rest. Osiyatech doesn't hold formal certifications such as SOC
2, HIPAA or ISO 27001. If your organisation must follow specific data protection regulations, the solution can be
designed to support those requirements and work with your compliance team, and the details are agreed during
assessment.
Can you automate our existing manual Excel and spreadsheet reports?
Yes. Osiyatech moves fragile, time-consuming spreadsheet reports into automated data pipelines and
interactive dashboards. The data is collected, cleaned and calculated automatically, and reports are always up
to date. This saves many hours of manual work each week, removes calculation and copy-paste errors, and
frees your team to analyse the numbers rather than assemble them.
Do we need to buy Power BI or Tableau licences? [drafted]
Not necessarily. If you already have licences for a BI tool, Osiyatech can build on it. If you'd rather avoid licence
costs, open-source options such as Metabase and Grafana, or a custom React dashboard, can deliver the
reporting you need. The team will explain the trade-offs so you can choose.

Can you build a data warehouse for us? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech designs and builds data warehouses on platforms such as Google BigQuery, Snowflake,
Amazon Redshift and PostgreSQL, fed by ETL pipelines built with tools such as Apache Airflow and dbt. A
warehouse brings data from all your systems into one structured place built for analysis, which makes reporting
faster, more consistent and easier to extend.
Can you add forecasting or predictive analytics? [drafted]
Yes. Predictive analytics and forecasting are part of the service, and they can be combined with Osiyatech's AI
& Machine Learning capabilities. Using your historical data, models can project trends such as sales, demand
or performance, and the results can be shown in the same dashboards your team already uses.
How much does a BI or analytics project cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the number and complexity of data sources, the
number of dashboards and reports, refresh requirements, whether a data warehouse is needed, and the tools
chosen. After a consultation, you'll receive a transparent estimate.
