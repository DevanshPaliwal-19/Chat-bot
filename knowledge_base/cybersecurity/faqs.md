# FAQs — Cybersecurity

What are cybersecurity services?
Cybersecurity services help organisations protect their applications, networks, cloud infrastructure and
sensitive information from unauthorised access, cyberattacks and security weaknesses. They include risk
assessments that find vulnerabilities, security testing such as penetration testing, preventive controls such as
access management and encryption, continuous monitoring, and incident preparedness so the organisation
can respond quickly if something happens.
What cybersecurity services does Osiyatech provide?
Osiyatech's cybersecurity offerings include vulnerability assessments, authorised penetration testing, web and
application security (including secure code reviews and API protection), cloud infrastructure security on AWS,
Azure and hybrid environments, network and infrastructure security, security configuration reviews and
hardening, compliance readiness assessments, and security monitoring and incident readiness support.
Engagements can be one-off assessments or ongoing support.
How does cybersecurity help protect cloud infrastructure?
Cloud environments are powerful but easy to misconfigure, which is a common cause of data breaches. Cloud
security combines identity and access management (controlling who can do what), encryption of data, network
controls, secure configuration settings, logging and vulnerability management. Together, these reduce the risks
to cloud-hosted applications and data. Osiyatech assesses your cloud setup against security best practices and
hardens it.
How often should businesses conduct cybersecurity assessments?
It depends on your level of risk, any regulatory requirements and how often your systems change. As a general
practice, periodic assessments are recommended, and additional assessments are advisable after major
application releases, significant infrastructure changes such as a cloud migration, or the introduction of new
business-critical systems. Osiyatech can help you set a sensible assessment schedule.
How can cybersecurity assessments improve business security?
An assessment shows you where you are actually exposed. It finds vulnerabilities, insecure configurations,
weaknesses in access control and other risks across your applications and infrastructure. The findings are
prioritised by risk, so you can fix the most serious issues first and invest your security budget where it matters
most. Over time, regular assessments build stronger security practices across the organisation.
What measures help prevent unauthorized access to business systems?
Key measures include multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, least-privilege permissions (giving
people only the access they need), secure password policies, network restrictions and segmentation, regular
patching of software and systems, and security monitoring to detect suspicious activity. Osiyatech can review
your current controls and help implement the ones that are missing.
Does Osiyatech provide ongoing cybersecurity support?
Yes. Ongoing support can include regular vulnerability reviews, security configuration updates, help with
remediation, monitoring integration and periodic security assessments. This keeps your controls effective as
your systems, threats and business change.
Do you perform penetration testing? [drafted]
Yes. Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing is a core offering. Penetration testing simulates real-world
attacks on your applications, networks and infrastructure, with your written authorisation and an agreed scope,
to find weaknesses an attacker could exploit. You receive clear findings with actionable recommendations for
fixing each issue.

Is Osiyatech certified, for example SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't currently hold formal third-party certifications such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA
attestation. Its security work follows established industry practices and frameworks, such as the OWASP
guidelines for application security, and uses widely recognised security tools. If your organisation needs to
meet a particular standard, Osiyatech can assess your systems against the relevant controls, help close the
gaps and work alongside your auditors. Formal certification of your organisation is issued by an accredited
auditor.
Can you help us prepare for SOC 2, ISO 27001 or other compliance requirements? [drafted]
Osiyatech offers compliance readiness assessments that review your applications and infrastructure against
the security controls such standards expect, identify gaps and help you put technical controls in place, such as
access management, encryption, logging and monitoring. Osiyatech isn't a certification body or auditor, so the
formal certification itself comes from an accredited auditor. Confirm the specific framework and scope with the
team.
How much do cybersecurity services cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the size and complexity of your environment, the type
of assessment or testing, the number of applications and systems in scope, and whether ongoing support is
needed. After a consultation, you'll receive a transparent estimate.
