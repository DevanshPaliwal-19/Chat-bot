# FAQs — Custom Software & Core Business Systems

> Source: Osiyatech Services Knowledge Base, Draft v3.0. Captured 2026-10-26. Source PDF pages 52–56.
> FAQ entries tagged `[drafted]` remain unapproved and must be reviewed.

Can you replace our spreadsheets with a proper system? [drafted]
Yes. This service exists to replace fragile spreadsheets and rigid off-the-shelf tools with custom ERP, CRM and
operations platforms designed around your workflows. Osiyatech first maps how your teams currently use
spreadsheets, including the formulas, hand-offs and workarounds, then builds a system that does the same
work reliably, with proper permissions, audit trails and one source of truth. Your historical spreadsheet data can
be cleaned and migrated into the new system.
What's the difference between this and buying an ERP or CRM product? [drafted]
A packaged ERP or CRM gives you standard features and expects your business to adapt to them, and you
often pay for modules you don't use. A custom system is built around your specific processes, includes only
what you need, and can change as your business changes. Osiyatech offers both routes, including
implementing and extending Microsoft Dynamics 365 (see ERP & CRM Solutions), and will recommend the
best fit after understanding your operations.
Do you work with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds custom Business Central extensions and modules, custom telemetry tables, Power
Platform connectors, third-party warehouse integrations and two-way synchronisation bridges between
Dynamics 365 and legacy ERPs. This lets you keep the strengths of Dynamics while tailoring it to processes it
doesn't cover out of the box.
How do you make sure the system fits how we work? [drafted]
Every project starts with a business process audit. Osiyatech's team shadows your staff to map workflow
bottlenecks, spreadsheet dependencies and integration needs, then designs the data structure and system
around what they learn. Modules are delivered step by step, and your team tests each one through user
acceptance testing and gives feedback before the next module begins. The system is shaped by the people
who will use it.
Will you move our existing data into the new system? [drafted]
Yes. Legacy data migration is a standard step. Historical company records are cleaned to remove duplicates
and errors, then migrated by script into the new system, with validation to confirm everything arrived correctly.
The migration is planned to avoid disrupting your daily operations.
Will you train our staff? [drafted]
Yes. Rollout includes staff onboarding, administrator training and system documentation, so your team is
confident using and managing the new system from day one. After launch, dedicated SLA support is available
for questions, fixes and improvements.
Is the system secure? [drafted]
Security is built in. Systems include fine-grained role-based permissions so each person sees and does only
what their role allows, automated audit trails that record who changed what, single sign-on (SSO)
authentication, and encryption of data at rest. Osiyatech doesn't hold formal certifications such as SOC 2, but
the system can be designed to support your organisation's security policies and compliance needs.
How much does custom business software cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the number of modules, the complexity of your
workflows, integrations and the volume of data migration. Because the system is delivered module by module,
you can prioritise the most valuable parts first. After a consultation and process review, you'll receive a
transparent, milestone-based estimate.
