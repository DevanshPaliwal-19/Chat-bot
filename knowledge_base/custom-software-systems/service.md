Service overview
Osiyatech's Custom Software & Core Business Systems service replaces rigid off-the-shelf software and fragile
spreadsheets with custom ERP, CRM and internal operations platforms built around how a company actually
works. The page title is "Custom Software & Core Business Systems Tailored to Your Workflows."
The service focuses on the systems a business runs on every day: centralised operations engines for
inventory, accounting, projects and permissions; bespoke CRMs and client portals; Microsoft Dynamics 365
Business Central extensions; and workflow engines that automate documents, approvals and contract signing.
Because the software is custom-built, businesses avoid unnecessary licence costs and features they don't use,
and get workflows that match their real processes.
The delivery approach is unusually hands-on. Osiyatech begins with a business process audit, shadowing the
client's teams to map bottlenecks and spreadsheet dependencies. It then delivers the system module by
module with user feedback, migrates historical data, and trains staff and administrators at rollout.
Business problems solved
•
Running the business on spreadsheets: fragile spreadsheets break, conflict and hide errors. A custom
platform gives one reliable system of record.
•
Rigid off-the-shelf software: packaged tools force workarounds and charge for unused features. Custom
systems fit actual workflows.
•
Fragmented SaaS tools: many disconnected subscriptions create data silos. A modular ERP gives a single
source of operational truth.
•
Manual approvals and paperwork: document generation, multi-level approvals and contract signing done
by hand slow operations. Workflow engines automate them, with audit logs.
•
Legacy desktop software: old monolithic systems are hard to access and maintain. They can be refactored
into secure, cloud-native web systems.
•
Disconnected accounting, billing, shipping and marketing tools: API integration and middleware keep
data in sync without loss or drift.
•
Security and permissions: fine-grained role-based permissions, audit trails, SSO and encryption at rest
protect business data.

Target customers and use cases
Custom Software & Core Business Systems serves companies whose day-to-day operations run on
spreadsheets, rigid packaged software or disconnected tools, and who want one system built around how they
actually work.
By company size
•
Startups and scale-ups: businesses that have grown fast on spreadsheets and basic SaaS tools and now
find them breaking down. A modular custom system lets them replace the most painful processes first and
add modules as they grow.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): the core audience for this service. These are businesses with
unusual workflows, such as specialist manufacturers, distributors and service companies, for whom
packaged ERP or CRM software is either too rigid or too expensive for what they use. Osiyatech's process
audit and staff training make the transition manageable for teams without in-house IT.
•
Enterprises and larger organisations: companies running Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or
legacy desktop software that need custom extensions, integration bridges or modernization into secure,
cloud-native systems.
By situation or need
•
Running the business on spreadsheets: teams whose critical information lives in fragile, conflicting
spreadsheets.
•
Fighting rigid packaged software: businesses that need custom workflows a standard product can't
provide.
•
Too many disconnected subscriptions: companies juggling several SaaS tools with no single source of
truth.
•
Manual approvals and paperwork: organisations that generate documents, chase approvals and sign
contracts by hand.
•
Extending Dynamics 365 Business Central: users who need custom modules, Power Platform connectors
or integrations with warehouses and legacy systems.

Development process
1
Business process audit: Osiyatech shadows the client's teams to map workflow bottlenecks, spreadsheet
dependencies and integration needs.
2
Data schema blueprint: relational database architecture, API contract design and a security compliance
roadmap.
3
Modular engineering: departmental modules delivered step by step, with continuous team feedback and
user acceptance testing.
4
Legacy data migration: historical company records are cleaned and migrated by script, aiming for zero
operational disruption.
5
Rollout and staff training: staff onboarding, administrator training, documentation and dedicated SLA
support.
Requirements and dependencies
•

for migration.

work with it. Where Microsoft Dynamics 365 is part of the solution, the client will need the relevant licences.

Delivery timeline: requires assessment.
