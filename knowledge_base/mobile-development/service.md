Service overview
Osiyatech's Mobile App Development service builds iOS and Android applications, from consumer apps to
enterprise mobile solutions. The tagline is "Mobile Experiences, Smartly Engineered." The service turns product
ideas into fluid, feature-rich apps using native technologies (Swift and SwiftUI for iOS, Kotlin and Java for
Android) or cross-platform frameworks (Flutter, React Native and Ionic) that serve both platforms from one
shared codebase.
The service covers the full mobile lifecycle: concept and architecture decisions, mobile UI/UX design,
engineering, backend and API integration, App Store and Google Play launch, and ongoing maintenance as
Apple and Google release new OS versions each year. Osiyatech has particular strength in offline-first apps
that store data on the device and sync with the cloud when a connection returns. This matters for field teams,
warehouses and anyone working with patchy connectivity.
Clients keep full ownership. Osiyatech states that clients retain 100% intellectual property ownership of the
source code, design systems and database schemas, and that apps are published under the client's own Apple
Developer and Google Play Console accounts.
Business problems solved
•
Reaching both iPhone and Android users without building twice: cross-platform development with
Flutter or React Native ships both apps from one shared codebase. The website says this can cut
time-to-market and engineering budget by up to 40%.
•
Apps that need deep device capabilities or maximum performance: native Swift and Kotlin development
suits apps that need close hardware integration, complex Bluetooth or AR features, or heavy computation.
•
Teams that work without reliable internet: offline-first apps store data locally in SQLite or Room
databases and sync automatically when a network connection is restored.
•
Apps that break or fall behind after OS updates: maintenance packages cover OS compatibility patches,
new device screen sizes, third-party SDK updates and security monitoring.
•
Disconnected customer experiences: booking apps, e-commerce and loyalty apps let customers order,
pay, track purchases and earn rewards from their phones.
•
Field and workforce inefficiency: enterprise apps for workforce management, inventory and field service,
connected to ERP and CRM systems, give staff the tools they need anywhere.
•
Failed or abandoned apps from previous developers: client testimonials describe Osiyatech rescuing a
failing app built by another developer and making admin workflows faster.

Technologies used
•
Cross-platform frameworks (one codebase for iOS and Android): Flutter, React Native, Ionic.
•
Native iOS and Android: Swift, SwiftUI, Kotlin, Java.
•
AR and VR: Apple ARKit (iOS) and Google ARCore (Android) for augmented reality, plus 3D content and
immersive frameworks agreed per project.
•
Backend and cloud infrastructure (APIs and server logic behind the app): Node.js, FastAPI, Firebase,
AWS, Microsoft Azure.
•
Databases and offline storage: PostgreSQL, MongoDB, SQLite, Room, Supabase.
•
Redis (fast backend performance): used on the app's backend for caching API responses, managing user
sessions, real-time features such as live updates and chat, and queuing push notifications and background
jobs.
•
DevOps, testing and on-device AI: Docker, GitHub Actions, Maestro (mobile UI testing), TensorFlow Lite
(machine learning on the device).
Target customers and use cases
Mobile App Development serves businesses that want to reach customers or equip staff through smartphones
and tablets, from first-time app builders to enterprises with large field teams.
By company size
•
Startups and founders: companies launching a mobile-first product and needing both iOS and Android
quickly and affordably. Osiyatech often recommends cross-platform development (Flutter or React Native) at
this stage to reach both platforms from one codebase, with an MVP typically taking 6 to 10 weeks. Founders
keep full ownership of the code and publish under their own store accounts, which matters when raising
investment.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): retailers, service providers and specialist firms that want an app to
sell, take bookings, build loyalty or give customers a convenient way to interact with them. Osiyatech
handles design, development, store submission and ongoing updates, so the business doesn't need its own
mobile team.
•
Enterprises: organisations that need secure apps for employees, field teams, warehouses and B2B
customers, often working offline and connected to ERP and CRM systems. Complex enterprise apps
typically take 3 to 5 months and benefit from SLA-backed maintenance.

Development process
1
Concept: evaluate native against cross-platform, plan offline-first data synchronization, design the local
data schema and define low-latency API contracts.
2
Mobile UI/UX: design thumb-friendly layouts, fluid micro-interactions and design systems that follow Apple
HIG and Google Material Design 3.
3
Engineering: build smooth 60fps native apps in Swift and Kotlin, or cross-platform apps in Flutter and
React Native, released in iterative bi-weekly sprints.
4
Testing: test across devices and OS versions before release (see QA & Automated Testing).
5
Launch: publish to the App Store and Google Play under the client's accounts.
6
Maintenance: ongoing OS updates, bug fixes, security monitoring and new features.
Requirements and dependencies
•
Customer inputs required: the client's own Apple Developer and Google Play Console accounts, because
apps are deployed under them. Other inputs are not specified on the website.

Technology or platform prerequisites: none required. Osiyatech recommends the most suitable

work with it.
•
Delivery timeline: an MVP or standard business app typically takes 6 to 10 weeks. Complex enterprise
apps with custom backend integrations, offline synchronization and multi-tier authentication typically take 3
to 5 months.

Pricing: requires a project assessment.
