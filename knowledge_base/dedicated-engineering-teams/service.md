Service overview
Osiyatech's Dedicated Engineering Teams service ("Dedicated Engineering Pods" on the homepage) gives
businesses senior developers and architects who join the client's daily standups and sprint rituals to speed up
the product roadmap. Clients get experienced engineering capacity without the time and cost of recruiting a full
in-house team.
There are two main ways to engage. A Dedicated Engineering Team is an autonomous pod of senior
developers, architects and QA engineers that ships the client's product features continuously. An Extended
Engineering Pod places senior developers and specialist architects directly inside the client's existing team, its
sprints and its standups. Clients can also hire specific specialists: web developers, app developers, AI/ML
experts, DevOps engineers, QA engineers and UI/UX designers.
This model suits long-running product work. Client testimonials describe engagements of four, five and eight or
more years, and one client describes more than 50 projects completed together.
Business problems solved
•
A roadmap moving slower than the business needs: in-house teams can't keep up. Dedicated senior
engineers add capacity quickly.
•
Missing specialist skills: AI/ML, DevOps, QA or UI/UX expertise is hard and slow to hire. Specialists join
for as long as they're needed.
•
Hiring cost and time: building an in-house team takes months. A dedicated pod can start contributing much
sooner.
•
Need for continuity: project-by-project vendors lose context. A stable dedicated team keeps product
knowledge over time.
•
Coordination overhead: embedded engineers work inside the client's existing rituals and tools, not
alongside them.

Target customers and use cases
Dedicated Engineering Teams serves organisations that need experienced engineering capacity on an ongoing
basis, either a whole team to build their product or specialists to strengthen the team they already have.
By company size
•
Startups and founders: companies with a product roadmap but no in-house engineering team, or a very
small one. A dedicated pod of senior developers, architects and QA can act as their engineering department,
shipping features continuously, at lower cost and much faster than recruiting a full team.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): businesses with a small in-house team that needs extra capacity
for a busy period, or specialist skills it doesn't have, such as mobile, AI, DevOps or QA. An Extended
Engineering Pod adds those engineers directly into the existing sprints.
•
Enterprises: organisations running several product streams or large modernization programmes that need
stable, long-term teams that build deep knowledge of their systems, working inside established processes
and tools.
By situation or need
•
Continuous product development: companies with an ongoing roadmap rather than a one-off project.
•
Hiring is too slow or expensive: businesses that need engineers now and can't wait months to recruit.
•
Missing specialist skills: teams needing AI/ML, DevOps, QA, UI/UX, mobile or AR/VR expertise.
•
Scaling up for a deadline: teams that need temporary extra capacity for a launch or major release.
•
Taking over a product: companies whose previous development partner has left and who need a team to
continue the work.

1

2
Choose a model: a Dedicated Engineering Team or an Extended Engineering Pod.
3
Onboard: engineers join your tools, standups and sprint rituals.
4
Deliver continuously: sprints, demos and updates, following Osiyatech's six-stage methodology.
