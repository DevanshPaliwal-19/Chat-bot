# FAQs — Dedicated Engineering Teams

> Source: Osiyatech Services Knowledge Base, Draft v3.0. Captured 2026-10-26. Source PDF pages 81–84.
> FAQ entries tagged `[drafted]` remain unapproved and must be reviewed.

Can I hire a dedicated developer or a full team? [drafted]
Yes. You can hire individual specialists or a full team. Available roles include web developers, app developers,
AI/ML experts, DevOps engineers, QA engineers and UI/UX designers, and engineers with AR/VR experience
are also available. Osiyatech matches the team's size and skills to your roadmap, and the team can grow or
change as your needs change.
What is the difference between a Dedicated Engineering Team and an Extended Engineering
Pod? [drafted]
A Dedicated Engineering Team is an autonomous pod of senior developers, architects and QA engineers that
takes responsibility for delivering your product features continuously. It suits companies without their own
engineering team, or a separate product stream. An Extended Engineering Pod embeds Osiyatech engineers
in your existing team, so they work in your sprints, attend your standups and follow your processes. It suits
companies that already have a team and need more capacity or specialist skills.
Will the engineers work in our tools and meetings? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech's engineers join your daily standups, sprint planning and other rituals, and work in your tools,
such as your code repository, project management board and communication channels. They work as part of
your team, not as a separate vendor, which keeps communication direct and context shared.
How do you ensure quality and continuity? [drafted]
Teams are made up of experienced, senior engineers, and work follows Osiyatech's disciplined process:
two-week sprints, working demos, peer code reviews, automated testing and clear documentation. Long-term
engagements let engineers build deep knowledge of your product, and several clients have worked with
Osiyatech for many years.
Can the team work with our existing technology stack? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech assigns engineers experienced in your existing stack, whether that's React, Node.js, Python,
.NET, Flutter, native mobile or something else. If you're building something new, the team can recommend a
stack instead.
How much does a dedicated team cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed rates. Cost depends on the roles, seniority, team size and length of the
engagement. After discussing your needs, you'll receive a transparent proposal.
