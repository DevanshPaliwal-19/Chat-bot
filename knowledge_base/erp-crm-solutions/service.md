Service overview
Osiyatech's ERP & CRM Solutions service unifies business processes, customer relationships and critical data
through scalable ERP and CRM platforms. The tagline is "Intelligent ERP & CRM Solutions, Built for Connected
Enterprises." The service covers custom ERP and CRM development, Microsoft Dynamics 365 implementation
and customisation, integration with other business applications, data migration from legacy platforms, and
ongoing support.
Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems manage internal operations such as finance, inventory,
procurement and supply chain. Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems manage customer
interactions, sales pipelines, marketing and service. Together they help a business run efficiently on connected
information. Osiyatech either builds custom ERP and CRM platforms or implements and extends established
ones, particularly Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central.
Many clients come to this service to move off an ageing system. Osiyatech migrates business records,
customer information, transaction history and operational data to modern platforms, with validation and testing.
A client testimonial describes a data migration from Pronto to Dynamics 365 that went smoothly from start to
finish.
Business problems solved
•
Operations spread across disconnected systems: finance, inventory, sales and service run in separate
tools. ERP and CRM centralise them with one set of data.
•
Poor visibility of business performance: without connected data, managers can't see the full picture.
Centralised platforms give real-time visibility.
•
Sales and customer information in silos: leads, deals and service histories get lost. A CRM manages the
pipeline, interactions and service in one place.
•
Outdated ERP or CRM platforms: legacy systems limit growth. Migration and modernization move data to
modern platforms while keeping business continuity.
•
Packaged software that doesn't fit: Dynamics 365 customisation and extensions, or a fully custom ERP or
CRM, match platforms to real processes.
•
Manual re-entry between systems: ERP and CRM integration with accounting, e-commerce, inventory and
payment systems keeps data synchronised automatically.

Target customers and use cases
ERP & CRM Solutions serves organisations that need their operations, finances and customer relationships
managed in connected, reliable systems, whether they are implementing their first ERP or CRM, customising
Microsoft Dynamics 365 or moving off an ageing platform.
By company size
•
Startups and growing companies: businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and basic tools for sales,
customers and inventory, and need their first proper CRM or ERP, either a configured platform or a lean
custom system that can expand.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): the largest group for this service. They include distributors,
retailers, manufacturers and service firms implementing or customising Dynamics 365 Business Central, or
needing a custom ERP or CRM that fits their processes and integrates with accounting, e-commerce and
payment systems.
•
Enterprises: organisations with multiple departments, warehouses or locations that need complex ERP and
CRM implementations, large-scale data migrations from legacy platforms, and integration with many other
systems, plus long-term support.
