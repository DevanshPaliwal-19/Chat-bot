What are ERP and CRM solutions?
ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) systems manage a company's internal operations, such as finance,
accounting, inventory, procurement, production and supply chain, in one connected system. CRM (Customer
Relationship Management) systems manage everything to do with customers: leads, sales pipelines,
interactions, marketing and service. When the two are connected, sales, operations and finance all work from
the same up-to-date information, which reduces errors and makes the business run more efficiently.
What ERP and CRM services does Osiyatech provide?

Does Osiyatech provide Microsoft Dynamics 365 services?
Yes. Osiyatech's Dynamics 365 services include Business Central implementation and customisation, custom

automation, data migration from legacy ERPs and ongoing technical support. A client testimonial describes a
smooth data migration from Pronto to Dynamics 365.
Can existing ERP and CRM systems integrate with other business applications?
Yes. ERP and CRM systems can be integrated with accounting software, e-commerce platforms, inventory and
warehouse tools, payment gateways, marketing platforms and other business applications. Integrations use
APIs, middleware and automated data synchronisation, so information such as orders, stock levels, invoices
and customer records flows between systems without manual re-entry.
Is it possible to migrate from an existing ERP or CRM platform?
Yes. Osiyatech migrates business records, customer information, transaction history and operational data from
legacy platforms to modern ERP or CRM systems. The migration is planned carefully: data is mapped, cleaned
and moved, then validated and tested to confirm accuracy, with the aim of minimising disruption to daily
operations. User training is provided so your team is ready to work in the new system.
Do you provide ongoing ERP and CRM maintenance?
Yes. Ongoing support includes troubleshooting, performance optimisation, security updates, system
enhancements, integration maintenance, user support and platform upgrades. As your business changes, the
system can be extended with new modules, workflows and reports to keep it aligned with your operations.

Should we build a custom ERP/CRM or use Dynamics 365? [drafted]
Both are valid, and the right choice depends on your business. An established platform like Dynamics 365 suits
companies whose processes are fairly standard and who value a proven product with regular vendor updates.
A custom ERP or CRM suits companies with unusual workflows, specific integration needs or a wish to avoid
paying for unused features. Osiyatech offers both and recommends an approach after assessing your
workflows, systems and goals. A combination, such as Dynamics with custom modules, is also common.
Will you train our team on the new system? [drafted]
Yes. Training is part of ERP and CRM projects, including legacy migrations. Your users and administrators
learn how to use and manage the system so the change goes smoothly and the new platform is actually
adopted.
How much does an ERP or CRM project cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the platform chosen, the number of modules, the
amount of customisation, integrations, the volume of data migration and training needs. Platform licences, such
as Microsoft licences, are separate from Osiyatech's services. After a consultation, you'll receive a transparent
estimate.
