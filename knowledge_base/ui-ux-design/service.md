Service overview
Osiyatech's UI/UX Design & Prototyping service creates human-centered digital experiences for websites, web
applications, SaaS platforms and iOS and Android apps. The tagline is "Design With Purpose. Built to Scale."
The service brings strategy, creativity and technology together to design seamless digital journeys that connect
people with products, with the aim of improving brand experience, customer retention and conversion.
UI (user interface) design covers how a product looks: layouts, components, typography, colour and visual
consistency. UX (user experience) design covers how a product works for its users: research into their needs,
the structure of information, user flows and how easy tasks are to complete. Osiyatech handles both, from user
research and wireframes to high-fidelity Figma design systems and clickable prototypes that are tested with
real users before any code is written.
A distinguishing feature of the service is that designs are built to be developed. Osiyatech designs with
engineering feasibility in mind: auto-layout that mirrors real CSS, a standard 8pt spacing scale, and design
tokens that map to Tailwind CSS, React or Flutter variables. It also runs live handoff walkthroughs with the
engineers who will build the product. Clients own all design files outright.
Business problems solved
•
Products that are confusing or hard to use: complex workflows and cluttered dashboards frustrate users.
Osiyatech researches user needs, maps journeys and simplifies navigation and forms.
•
Low conversion and engagement: UX audits and conversion-focused improvements find where users
drop off and fix the friction.
•
Inconsistent design across products and teams: without a design system, every screen looks slightly
different. Osiyatech builds Figma component libraries, design tokens and style guides that keep products
consistent.
•
Outdated interfaces: older websites and apps look dated and lose trust. UI/UX Redesign modernizes
interfaces while keeping the functionality users rely on.
•
Redesigns that alienate existing users: sudden, jarring changes upset power users. Osiyatech uses
progressive modernization, rolling changes out step by step.
•
Designs that don't translate into code: a beautiful mockup that developers can't build accurately wastes
time. Osiyatech's engineering-aware design process and developer handoff remove that ambiguity.
•
Building the wrong thing: interactive prototypes and usability testing check ideas with real users before
money is spent on development.

Development process
Osiyatech follows the Double Diamond method, a widely used design approach that first explores the problem
broadly, then defines it, then develops and refines a solution.
1
Discovery and user research (weeks 1 to 2): understand users, business goals, personas and current
pain points.
2
Information architecture and wireframing (weeks 2 to 3): structure content and features, map user flows
and create low-fidelity wireframes.
3
High-fidelity UI design systems (weeks 3 to 5): create polished visual designs and the reusable
component system.
4
Interactive prototyping and usability validation (weeks 5 to 6): build clickable prototypes, test them with
users and refine.
5
Developer handoff: live walkthroughs with the engineering team and complete specifications.
