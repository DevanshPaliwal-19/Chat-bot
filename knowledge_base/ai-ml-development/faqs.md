What AI and machine learning development services do you offer?
Osiyatech provides end-to-end custom AI and machine learning development. That includes LLM-powered
applications and AI chatbots, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over your own data, generative AI,
predictive analytics and forecasting, natural language processing (NLP), computer vision and object detection,
document intelligence, and integrating AI models into existing systems. Projects run from the initial data and
opportunity assessment through model development, deployment and ongoing optimisation, so you get a

Can you develop custom AI solutions for our business?
Yes. Every AI solution is designed around your business objectives, your existing data and your operational
requirements. Osiyatech starts by understanding the problem you want to solve and checking whether your
data can support it, then recommends the most practical approach, whether that is an intelligent application, a
predictive model, a computer vision system or a combination. This keeps the focus on measurable business
value instead of AI for its own sake.
What is the difference between generative AI and traditional machine learning?
Traditional machine learning finds patterns in data to make predictions or classifications, for example
forecasting next month's sales, detecting fraud or recognising objects in an image. Generative AI creates new
content, such as written answers, summaries, emails or descriptions, using models like large language models
(LLMs). The two often work best together: a business application might use machine learning to predict which
customers need attention and generative AI to draft a personalised message to each one.
Can AI models be trained using our company's data?
Yes. There are two main approaches, and Osiyatech recommends the right one for your situation. Fine-tuning
adapts an existing AI model using your data, so it learns your terminology, tone or specialised tasks.
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) connects an AI model to your documents and databases, so it looks
up relevant information before answering, which keeps answers accurate and current without retraining. The
right choice depends on your data, use case, accuracy needs and budget, and the two can be combined.
Can you integrate AI and machine learning into our existing applications?
Yes. AI can be added to your existing websites, mobile apps, enterprise software and business platforms
through APIs and custom integrations, usually without rebuilding the whole system. For example, a chatbot can
be added to your current website, document processing can feed your existing ERP, or a forecasting model can
feed your current dashboards. This lets you gain AI capabilities while protecting your existing investment.
How long does it take to develop an AI or machine learning solution?
It depends on project complexity, how available and clean your data is, the model requirements and the
integrations needed. A basic proof of concept may take a few weeks and is a good way to confirm value early.
A production-ready solution takes longer, because it needs thorough development, testing, deployment,
monitoring and optimisation for real-world use. You'll receive a clear roadmap after the discovery and data
strategy stage.
Can you build object detection or computer vision systems? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds computer vision and object detection systems using models such as YOLOv8, for
example a utility meter reading system that detects meters and their reading areas in photos so readings no

images and video. Models can run in the cloud or directly on mobile devices, depending on speed and

Can you build an AI chatbot that answers questions from our own documents? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds AI chatbots and conversational assistants powered by large language models, and uses
RAG so answers come from your own documents, databases and enterprise content. That makes the chatbot
far more accurate and relevant than a generic AI. The chatbot can be deployed on your website, inside your
app, on messaging channels or internally for staff. See also the AI Agents & Workflow Automation service.
Is our data kept private when using AI? [drafted]
Data privacy is taken seriously in every AI project. Osiyatech offers LLM fine-tuning and private deployment,
with secure hosting options and controlled access to sensitive information, so confidential data can stay within
controlled infrastructure. Access controls, encryption and careful data handling are applied according to your
requirements. Osiyatech doesn't hold formal certifications such as SOC 2 or HIPAA, but if you have specific
regulatory or data-residency requirements, the solution can be designed to support them, and the details are
agreed during assessment.

Which AI models and frameworks do you work with? [drafted]
For generative AI, Osiyatech works with OpenAI models, Hugging Face, LangChain and LlamaIndex. For
machine learning and deep learning, it uses PyTorch, TensorFlow and Scikit-learn. For computer vision, it uses
YOLOv8 and TensorFlow Lite for on-device models. For vector search, it uses Pinecone and pgvector. Redis is
used for caching and conversation memory. For deployment, the stack includes FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes,
Kafka and AWS. If you have a preferred model provider or platform, Osiyatech can work with it.
Can AI power augmented reality features? [drafted]
Yes. Computer vision is what lets AR apps recognise surfaces, objects and environments so digital content can
be placed accurately in the real world. Osiyatech combines its computer vision work with its mobile and UI/UX
teams to build AR features, such as product visualisation, guided inspections and interactive training.
How much does an AI project cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the type of AI solution, the state of your data, model
complexity, integrations and hosting requirements (for example, private deployment). Many clients start with a
proof of concept, which keeps the initial investment focused and shows value before a full build. After a
consultation, you'll receive a transparent estimate.
