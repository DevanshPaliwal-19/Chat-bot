# FAQs — Web Development (Websites & Web Applications)

> Source: Osiyatech Services Knowledge Base, Draft v3.0. Captured 2026-10-26. Source PDF pages 7–13.

What web application development services does Osiyatech offer?
Osiyatech offers the full range of web development: custom websites, web applications, SaaS platforms,
customer portals, enterprise web portals, e-commerce stores, CMS-based websites, API development,
third-party integrations and modernization of existing applications. Projects can be as focused as a
high-converting landing page or as large as a multi-tenant SaaS product or an enterprise system used across
departments. Every solution is designed to be secure and scalable, and is tailored to your business
requirements rather than built from a generic template. Osiyatech can also add advanced features such as AI
chatbots, 3D and AR product experiences, and real-time functionality where they add value.
Which technologies do you use for web application development?
Osiyatech's web expertise includes React.js, Next.js, Vue.js and Angular on the frontend; Node.js, Python
(Django), PHP (Laravel), Go, Java Spring and .NET on the backend; and databases including PostgreSQL,
MySQL, Microsoft SQL Server, MongoDB, Supabase and Firebase. Redis is used for caching, sessions and
queues to keep applications fast under load. E-commerce and content projects use Shopify, Magento,
WooCommerce and WordPress. The team recommends a stack based on your performance needs, scalability
goals and long-term maintainability. If you already have a preferred or existing stack, Osiyatech can work with
it.

How long does it take to develop a custom web application?
It depends on the application's complexity, the number of features, the integrations needed and the design
requirements. As a guide, a basic MVP may take 4 to 8 weeks, while more complex business applications can
take 3 to 6 months or longer. Larger platforms are often delivered in phases, so you can launch core features
first and add more over time. You'll receive a detailed timeline with milestones after your requirements are
reviewed, and you'll see working demos every other Friday during development.
Can you modernize or upgrade an existing web application?
Yes. Osiyatech regularly modernizes legacy websites and web applications. That can mean improving
performance, redesigning the interface, moving to a modern technology stack, refactoring old code, integrating
new APIs, adding new features, or migrating to the cloud. The team first reviews your current system to
understand what works, what doesn't and what the business depends on, then plans the upgrade so critical
operations keep running throughout. Client testimonials include rescuing applications left in poor shape by
previous developers.
Will my web application be secure, scalable and mobile-friendly?
Yes, these are built in from the start. Every interface is designed to be responsive, so it works well on desktop,
tablet and mobile. Depending on your requirements, security measures include secure authentication,
role-based access control, API security and protection of sensitive data. For scalability, Osiyatech uses
cloud-ready architecture, database optimisation, caching with Redis and, where appropriate, microservices, so
the application can handle growth in users and data. Automated testing and CI/CD pipelines help keep quality
consistent as the application evolves.
Do you provide maintenance and support after launch?
Yes. Every project includes post-launch support, and Osiyatech offers ongoing maintenance plans afterwards.
A plan can include bug fixes, performance monitoring, security updates, framework and dependency upgrades,
feature enhancements and technical support. The scope of each plan is agreed with you, so you get the level of
support your business needs, from occasional updates to continuous development with a dedicated team.
Can you build an online store on Shopify, Magento or WooCommerce? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds e-commerce stores on Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce, including theme
customisation, payment gateway integration, and product and order management. When an off-the-shelf
platform can't support your requirements, such as complex pricing, custom checkout flows or deep ERP
integration, the team builds a fully custom e-commerce solution. Stores are designed to convert visitors into
buyers, and can include features such as 3D product views.
Do you build SaaS platforms? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds SaaS platforms and customer portals, including multi-tenant SaaS products where many
customer organisations share one platform with their data kept separate. A SaaS build typically covers user
and subscription management, role-based access, admin dashboards, reporting, APIs and integrations, and
the cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines needed to release updates safely. Osiyatech can support a SaaS
product from MVP through to scale.
Can my team update the website content without a developer? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech builds websites on WordPress, custom CMS platforms or headless CMS solutions, so your
marketing and content teams can create, edit and publish pages, posts, images and other content themselves.
A headless CMS also lets the same content appear on your website, mobile app and other channels. Osiyatech
can migrate existing content and customise the CMS to match how your team works.
Can you integrate my website with payment gateways, CRM or ERP systems? [drafted]
Yes. API and third-party integrations are a core part of web application development, and ERP and CRM
integration is part of Osiyatech's enterprise web solutions. Common integrations include payment gateways,
CRMs, ERPs, accounting software, shipping and marketing tools. For large or complex integration projects
involving many systems, see the Enterprise System Integration service.

Can you add 3D or AR experiences to our website? [drafted]
Yes. Osiyatech can build interactive 3D and augmented reality experiences that run in the browser, for example
letting customers rotate a product in 3D or preview it in their own space through their phone camera. These
experiences are especially useful for e-commerce, real estate and product showcase websites. The approach
and technology are agreed during project assessment, based on your products and audience.
How much does a website or web application cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices, because cost depends on the type of project, the number of features
and pages, integrations, design complexity and the engagement model. A focused website costs very
differently from a multi-tenant SaaS platform. After an initial consultation, you'll receive a transparent,
milestone-based estimate so you know exactly what you're paying for at each stage.
