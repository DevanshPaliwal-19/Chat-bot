Service overview
Osiyatech's Web Development service designs, builds and maintains websites and web applications for
businesses of every size. Projects range from a fast, search-friendly corporate website that wins customers to
complex web applications that run day-to-day operations: customer portals, admin dashboards, multi-tenant
SaaS platforms and enterprise web systems. The service tagline is "Web Applications, Designed to Deliver."
The service covers the full web lifecycle. Osiyatech plans the technical roadmap, chooses frameworks, designs
the user interface, builds the frontend and backend, integrates third-party systems, sets up automated
deployment, and supports the application after launch. Applications are built on modern frameworks such as
React, Next.js, Node.js and Python, and are made easy for the client's own team to update.
Osiyatech builds new web products and modernizes existing ones. A business with an outdated, slow or
insecure website or web application can have it redesigned, refactored, moved to a newer technology stack or
migrated to the cloud, without disrupting the operations that depend on it.
Business problems solved
•
Manual or scattered business processes: teams run operations through spreadsheets, email and
disconnected tools. Osiyatech builds custom web applications and portals that centralise information,
automate workflows and give customers and employees one place to work.
•
A website that doesn't win customers: a slow, outdated or poorly structured site loses visitors and leads.
Osiyatech builds fast, responsive, search-friendly websites and landing pages designed to convert.
•
Legacy web applications that hold the business back: older applications become hard to maintain, slow
and insecure. Website Modernization covers redesign, legacy upgrades, performance optimization and
platform migration while keeping critical operations running.
•
Selling online without a reliable store: businesses need secure payments, easy product management and
a smooth checkout. Osiyatech builds e-commerce on Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce, or fully custom
stores.
•
Content that is hard to manage: marketing teams depend on developers for every content change.
Osiyatech builds WordPress, custom and headless CMS solutions so non-technical staff can publish content
themselves.
•
Systems that don't talk to each other: web platforms often need to share data with ERP, CRM, payment
and other tools. Osiyatech builds APIs and third-party integrations to connect them.
•
Growth that outpaces the platform: a product that works for a few hundred users can fail at scale.
Osiyatech plans for cloud scalability, uses modular and microservice architectures, and sets up CI/CD so the
platform can grow safely.

Features and capabilities
The service is organised into six offerings.
1. Custom Website Development — High-Performance Digital Flagships
Modern, responsive websites that showcase a business, engage visitors and work smoothly on every device.
•
Business and corporate websites: professional sites that present the company, its services and its brand.

Responsive website development: layouts that adapt to desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
•
Custom landing pages: focused pages built to convert visitors for campaigns, products or lead generation.

Website performance optimization: faster load times and better overall site performance.

Secure, scalable web applications that simplify business processes, manage information and give customers
and employees a seamless experience.
•
Custom business web applications: software built around a company's own workflows.

SaaS platforms and customer portals: subscription products and self-service portals, including
multi-tenant SaaS.
•
Admin dashboards and reporting: internal control panels that show data and let staff manage operations.
•
API and third-party integrations: connecting the application to payment gateways, CRMs, ERPs and other

3. E-Commerce Development — Conversion-Tuned Digital Commerce
User-friendly online stores with secure payments, easy product management and smooth shopping
experiences.
•
Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce: stores built on established e-commerce platforms.
•
Custom e-commerce development: tailored stores for businesses whose needs go beyond off-the-shelf
platforms.
•
Payment gateway integration: secure online payment processing.
•
Product and order management: tools to manage catalogues, inventory, orders and fulfilment.
4. Enterprise Web Solutions — Mission-Critical Cloud Infrastructure
Custom enterprise web applications that streamline operations, connect departments and manage complex
workflows securely.

Enterprise application development: large web systems for organisation-wide use.
•
ERP and CRM integration: connecting web platforms with enterprise resource planning and customer
relationship management systems.
•
Business workflow automation: automating approvals, hand-offs and repetitive processes.

Role-based access and user management: controlling who can see and do what inside the application.

Flexible content management that makes it simple to create, edit and manage website content while keeping

WordPress website development: sites built on the widely used WordPress CMS.

Custom CMS development: bespoke content management tailored to specific publishing needs.

Headless CMS integration: separating content management from presentation, so the same content can
be published to websites, apps and other channels.

CMS customization and migration: extending an existing CMS or moving content to a new one.

Updated designs, faster performance, newer technology and stronger security for existing websites and web

Website redesign and revamp: a refreshed look and improved usability.

Legacy website upgrades: moving outdated code and frameworks to current, supported technology.

Technologies used
Osiyatech recommends a technology stack for each project based on its requirements, performance needs and
scalability goals. If the client already has a preferred stack, Osiyatech can work with it.
•
Frontend (what users see and interact with): React.js, Next.js, Vue.js, Angular, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS.
•
Backend and APIs (business logic, data processing, integrations): Node.js, Python with Django, PHP
with Laravel, Go (Golang), Java Spring, GraphQL and .NET.
•
Databases and storage (how application data is stored and retrieved): PostgreSQL, MongoDB, MySQL,
Microsoft SQL Server, Supabase, Firebase.
•
Redis (speed and scalability): an in-memory data store used for caching frequently accessed data, storing
user sessions, rate limiting, background job queues and real-time features. It helps web applications
respond quickly under heavy traffic.
•
E-commerce and CMS platforms: Shopify, Magento, WooCommerce, WordPress, plus headless CMS
options.
•
Cloud and DevOps (hosting, deployment, scaling): Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud,
Microsoft Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions.
•
3D and AR on the web: browser-based 3D and AR technologies such as WebXR and Three.js, for product
visualisation and immersive experiences.
•
AI-assisted development and design tools: v0 by Vercel, Cursor AI, GitHub Copilot, Lovable, Bolt.new,
Figma AI.

operations. The common thread is a business that needs a reliable, scalable web presence or web-based tool
built around its own goals.

Startups and founders: early-stage companies that need to turn an idea into a working product quickly,
usually an MVP web application or SaaS product they can show investors and early users. Osiyatech helps
them choose a stack that won't need a rewrite as they grow, builds the MVP in focused sprints (a basic MVP
typically takes 4 to 8 weeks), and stays on to add features after launch. A Fixed Scope Milestones
engagement often suits this stage.
•
Small and medium businesses (SMEs): established businesses that need a professional website to win
customers, an online store to sell directly, or a custom web tool to replace spreadsheets and manual
processes. These clients often have no in-house developers, so they value Osiyatech handling everything
from design to hosting and maintenance, and building sites their own staff can update through a CMS.

Development process
1
Strategy: Osiyatech builds a technical roadmap, plans for cloud scalability and selects frameworks that fit
the client's workflows and long-term goals.
2
Design: the team creates intuitive, conversion-focused interfaces and component systems that remove
friction across desktop, tablet and mobile.
3
Development: engineers build modular frontends, resilient microservices, secure database models and
automated CI/CD pipelines. Work runs in two-week sprints with working demos every other Friday.
4
Validation: automated tests, peer code reviews, load benchmarks and hands-on QA happen before
release.
5
Launch: zero-downtime production deployment through automated pipelines, with rollback readiness.
