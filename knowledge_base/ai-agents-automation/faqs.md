# FAQs — AI Agents & Workflow Automation

What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent? [drafted]
An AI chatbot mainly holds a conversation: it understands a question and gives an answer, often drawing on
your documents. An AI agent goes further and takes action. It can work through a multi-step task, decide what
to do next, look up information in your systems, update records, trigger workflows and hand the case to a
person when needed. Multi-agent systems use several specialised agents working together on complex
processes. Osiyatech builds all of these and recommends the right level for your needs.
What kinds of tasks can AI agents automate? [drafted]
AI agents are well suited to repetitive, rules-guided work that involves reading, deciding and acting. Examples
include answering common customer questions, triaging and routing incoming requests, collecting details from
customers before a human takes over, looking up information across systems, updating CRM records,
generating summaries or follow-up messages, and coordinating multi-step back-office processes. During
assessment, Osiyatech helps identify which of your workflows will benefit most.
Can you build a chatbot that answers from our own documents? [drafted]
Yes. Knowledge-base and document chatbots are a core offering. They use retrieval-augmented generation
(RAG): when someone asks a question, the system finds the relevant passages in your documents and uses
them to write an accurate answer. This grounds responses in your actual content, such as manuals, policies,
product information or FAQs, instead of the AI's general knowledge, and the knowledge base can be updated
as your content changes.

Can an AI assistant work on WhatsApp or connect to our CRM? [drafted]
Yes. WhatsApp API is part of the technology stack, so assistants can talk to customers directly on WhatsApp.
CRM and third-party integrations are a listed capability, which lets agents read and update customer
information, log conversations and pass qualified leads or cases to your team. The exact integrations for your
systems are confirmed during project assessment.
Can you automate our customer support? [drafted]
Yes. Customer support automation uses AI chatbots and agents to answer common enquiries instantly, at any
hour, and to collect the details needed to resolve more complex issues. When a question needs a person, the
conversation is handed to your support team with the full context, so customers don't have to repeat
themselves. The result is faster responses for customers and less repetitive work for your team.
Will the AI hand conversations over to a human when needed? [drafted]
Yes. Good automation knows its limits. Agents and chatbots can be designed to escalate to a human when a
question falls outside what they should handle, when a customer asks for a person, or when a situation is
sensitive. The handover rules are agreed with you during design, so automation supports your team rather than
replacing judgement where it matters.
How do you make sure the AI gives accurate answers? [drafted]
Accuracy comes from grounding the AI in your approved content through RAG, setting clear instructions on
what it should and shouldn't answer, and testing it thoroughly with real questions before launch. After launch,
conversations can be monitored and the knowledge base and instructions refined over time. Osiyatech's AI
development process includes model validation and continuous optimisation for this reason.
Is our data secure when using AI agents? [drafted]
Data security is built into the design. Depending on your requirements, this can include secure authentication,
role-based access to connected systems, encryption of data in transit and at rest, and private deployment
options for sensitive information. Agents are given only the access they need to do their job. Osiyatech doesn't
hold formal certifications such as SOC 2 or HIPAA, but solutions can be designed to support your
organisation's security and compliance requirements.
How long does it take to build an AI agent or automation? [drafted]
It depends on the complexity of the workflow, the number of systems to connect and how ready your
knowledge sources are. A focused chatbot or single-task agent can often be delivered as a proof of concept
relatively quickly, while multi-agent systems that span several systems take longer to design, build and test.
You'll receive a timeline after the assessment.
Which technologies do you use for AI agents? [drafted]
Osiyatech builds agents with OpenAI models and the LangChain framework, using Python, Node.js and
FastAPI for the backend, the WhatsApp API for messaging, RAG pipelines for knowledge retrieval, and Redis
for conversation memory, caching and reliable task queues. If you have a preferred AI provider or platform,
Osiyatech can work with it.
How much does an AI automation project cost? [drafted]
Osiyatech doesn't publish fixed prices. Cost depends on the workflows being automated, the number of
channels and integrations, the size of the knowledge base and the AI complexity. Starting with one high-impact
workflow is a common way to prove value quickly. After a consultation, you'll receive a transparent estimate.
